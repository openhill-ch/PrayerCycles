Privacy Policy for Watchful Prayer

Last updated: 2 July 2026

Watchful Prayer (“the app”) is designed to be private by default. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

The short version

Watchful Prayer does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any of your data. Everything you enter — prayer lists, prayers, descriptions, and tags — stays on your device. There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics, and no third-party tracking.

What data the app stores

The app stores the following locally on your device only:

  • Prayer lists and their settings (cadence, schedule, tags)
  • Individual prayers, including titles, descriptions, and tags
  • App preferences (theme, language, sound, and similar settings)

This information is saved in your device’s local storage. It is encrypted at rest using a key stored in your device’s secure storage (Apple Keychain on iOS). The app cannot read your data without that key, and the key never leaves your device except through Apple’s own iCloud Keychain sync, if you have that enabled.

What the app does NOT do

  • It does not send your data to any server.
  • It does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking tools.
  • It does not require an account or any personal information.
  • It does not share your data with third parties.

Bible verse feature

The app includes a King James Version (KJV) Bible text bundled inside the app for the verse-autocomplete feature. Looking up a verse happens entirely on your device — no network request is made, and your searches are not recorded or sent anywhere.

Backups

You can export your data to a backup file from within the app. This file is created on your device and is yours to keep. The app does not upload it anywhere. Where you choose to store that backup file (for example, in iCloud Drive or another service) is governed by that service’s own privacy policy, not this one.

Children’s privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new “Last updated” date.
